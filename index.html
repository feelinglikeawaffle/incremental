Automation Workshop

Build a production line by connecting machine nodes. Click a green output pin, then a white input pin. Drag node headers to arrange the diagram; select a node to read its purpose and settings. Your graph is saved with your game.

Quick guide
  1. Add a node from the palette.
  2. Click its green output, then the next node’s white input.
  3. Connect a route to Sell Ore to enable automatic sales.
  4. Click a wire to remove it; select nodes to edit settings.
Port key: green = output; white = input; amber = pending connection.
